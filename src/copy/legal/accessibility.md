Last updated: 30 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to find, compare and book a speaker on this website, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they use.

### What we aim for

We design and build the site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. In practice that means:

- every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a link to skip straight to the main content
- headings, landmarks and form labels are marked up so screen readers can announce them
- text and controls have enough colour contrast to read comfortably, and nothing relies on colour alone
- photos of speakers carry text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from assistive technology
- moving content, such as the home page carousel and rotating headline, stops when you ask your device to reduce motion, and the carousel pauses when you hover over it or move into it with the keyboard
- pages still work when you zoom to 400% or enlarge text

### Known limitations

Some content comes from partners, such as the venue-hire pages for event spaces, which open on their own websites and follow their own accessibility statements. Embedded photos come from licensed picture libraries and their descriptions are written by us.

### Tell us about a problem

If something on the site does not work for you, or you need information in another format, send us a message through the enquiry form and tell us the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within one working day, and we use reports to fix problems for everyone.

### How we test

We check new pages with automated tools and by hand, using a keyboard and a screen reader, before they go live, and we review this statement whenever the site changes significantly.
